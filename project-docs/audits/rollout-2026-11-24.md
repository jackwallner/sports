## Good moments (Rollout S)

Gates unchanged: no ask before the 2nd cold launch, 30-day cooldown stamped when
the call is made, opening the write-review page ends the passive asks, feedback
never does, never in onboarding. The call waits 3.5 s, then re-checks that the
briefing is on screen with no sheet (source, paywall or Pro preview) up and the
deck is not on the rooms pitch card; a blocked ask stays pending for the next
moment or a source sheet's dismissal. Settings keeps "Rate Gist" on
`?action=write-review`. The app has no analytics capture call, so `rating_asked`
is n/a.

1. A fresh briefing loads from a pull-to-refresh or a room tap. A saved copy,
   a failed load and a paywall-driven room open never count. Why: the user asked
   for a briefing and just got a live one. How often: a free user can refresh
   once a day and tapping a room reloads it, so most users who open the app a
   second time hit it in session 2 or 3. Pro users refresh without a limit.
2. The deck reaches its "Your move" card (the briefing read through). Why: the
   core action, reading today's briefing, is finished. How often: every user who
   swipes the deck to its end, so most users in sessions 2 and 3. The app has no
   analytics, so these frequencies are judgment from the flow, not measured.

## Remote config notes

- Control text is today's copy: paywall "Start My 7-Day Free Trial" (the length
  comes from the intro offer, capitalised as before), sheet "Try <Room> Free".
  `my` and `your` read "Start My Free Trial" and "Start Your Free Trial" on both
  title-case surfaces.
- Trial-ineligible wording is unchanged for every arm ("Subscribe" on the
  paywall, "Unlock <Room>" on the sheet). Lifetime stays "Unlock Lifetime".
- The app has no onboarding trial page, so only the paywall and the Pro preview
  sheet are routed. The text links "Try Pro Free" (caught-up screen and banner)
  and the deck's "Try every room free" open the paywall rather than start a
  trial, so they keep their copy. Say if you want those in the test too.
- The app has no string catalog and ships English only, so there are no
  translations to add.
- The reader uses `offerings.current.metadata`, not `currentOffering` (which
  prefers the offering named `default`), so a running Experiment's variant is
  read.
- `Sideline.xcodeproj` was not regenerated: `xcodegen` is not installed on the
  iMac. The GitHub runner runs `xcodegen generate` itself. Run it on the Mac
  before the next build.
