## Executive directive

Keep the consumer product brand `Gist` and App Store title `Gist: Sports Made Simple`. Replace the subtitle with `Daily Briefing for Non-Fans` and replace the US hidden keyword field with the exact 97-byte field below. The title already protects the brand and the strongest currently indexed descriptive phrase. The new subtitle adds the format, cadence, and intended audience without wasting another visible occurrence of `sports`.

The product should own a narrow job: help someone who does not closely follow sports understand one current story, say one useful thing, answer a follow-up, and ask a natural question. It is not a scores app, live game product, fantasy assistant, betting tool, trivia game, or dense sports news feed. Do not trade this differentiation for high-volume but unqualified news and scores traffic.

They are the five largest observed non-US sources of search impressions, but none has a complete acquisition funnel. The Saudi spike in particular is exposure, not proof of demand. ### Why this subtitle wins

`Daily Briefing for Non-Fans` uses each visible term for a separate job:

- `Daily` states the cadence already described by the free product.
- `Briefing` names the implemented content format and protects the current `sports briefing` discovery.
- `Non-Fans` defines the audience and immediately distinguishes Gist from expert news, scores, fantasy, and betting apps.
- The title supplies `Sports`, so Apple can compose `sports briefing`, `daily sports briefing`, `sports for non-fans`, and `sports made simple` across indexed fields.

Do not use `Sports Briefing for Non-Fans`. It repeats `Sports`, which is already in the title. Do not use `Talking Points for Non-Fans` as the primary subtitle. `talking points` has a high Astro estimate but a polluted education and politics result set, while Gist remains unranked for the generic query. Pursue it only as the qualified compound `sports talking points` through hidden tokens and screenshot copy.

## Wrong-intent and authority walls

Do not target or claim:

- live scores, schedules, standings, play-by-play, streams, alerts, or real-time game tracking
- sports betting, odds, picks, props, parlays, sportsbooks, or predictions
- fantasy lineups, drafts, projections, start/sit decisions, waiver advice, or DFS
- trivia, quizzes, games, cards, or party-game icebreakers
- official league, team, athlete, broadcast, or news-source affiliation
- exhaustive news coverage, breaking news, exclusive reporting, or original reporting
- Local Team, five contexts, or a refresh cadence that the native app does not implement
- team marks, league marks, athlete photography, or third-party publication branding without clearance

`news` and `highlights` may describe adjacent content, but they attract users who expect a broad feed or video. Gist should earn qualified discovery through briefing, explained, recap, catch-up, and conversation terms.
