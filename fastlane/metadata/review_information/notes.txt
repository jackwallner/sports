App Store listing name: Sports Cheat Sheet: Daily News. Internal app and purchase labels may retain the name Gist.

Gist provides short sports briefings with background, cited sources and conversation starters. Briefing content is in English; the App Store metadata is localized. No account or permissions are required. Internet access is needed to fetch briefings.

What changed in 1.1.6 (new build): when a fresh briefing cannot be fetched, the app shows the saved copy with its age instead of calling it offline, backs off during upstream outages, keeps a free user's daily refresh, and shows Pro users the real next refresh time. Reduce Motion now skips the card throw and card art fades, and relaunch cross-fades instead of hard-cutting. Onboarding buttons no longer shift when Back appears.

First launch: Start > review the free Cocktail Party room > See today's briefing. Read the swipeable story cards, open a story for context and its source, and finish with the conversation prompt. The free room refreshes once daily.

Gist Pro unlocks Work, Family and Date Night rooms, with scheduled refreshes up to three times daily. After finishing the free briefing, use Try every room free, or open a locked room and its sample. Settings also includes Gist Pro and Restore Purchases. The paywall is dismissible and shows available plans, localized prices and terms. Monthly and annual subscriptions have a one-week trial when eligible. Test purchases using an Apple sandbox account.

There is no user-generated content, betting advice, fantasy advice or live-score service. Privacy and Apple Standard EULA links are available in Settings, the paywall and every localized store description.
