No account, login, or permissions are required. There is no user-generated content. All briefing content is generated by our backend from cited news sources and refreshed on a schedule.

WHAT YOU WILL SEE
1. First launch: a 2-page onboarding. Tap "Start", review the free Cocktail Party room, then tap "See today's briefing".
2. Main screen: a horizontal rail of room chips above a swipeable card deck. Swipe cards left or right. The first card gives the essential story, story cards can be tapped for the backstory and source, and the final question card gives one conversation prompt.
3. Free users see a Gist Pro rooms card only after finishing the free briefing. The app does not present a paywall before the reviewer can experience the core product.

IN-APP PURCHASE
- Path: finish the free briefing and tap "Try every room free". Or tap any locked room, review its sample, then tap "Try <room> Free". The gear icon also contains a Gist Pro row.
- The paywall lists Annual, Monthly, and Lifetime plans. Annual is selected by default. Eligible Annual and Monthly plans include a one-week free trial.
- The purchase point shows localized price, duration, trial terms, automatic renewal disclosure, Restore Purchases, Apple Standard EULA, and Privacy Policy links.
- Please test with a sandbox Apple ID.
