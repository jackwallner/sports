Sports Cheat Sheet is a daily sports news briefing for people who don't follow sports but want to keep up when it comes up. In about five minutes you get what happened, why people care, and something to say. The daily briefing is free, with no account.

THE DAILY BRIEFING
• A short deck of the sports stories people are talking about, one card each
• Each card leads with one line you can say, and you tap for the backstory
• Every story links to the news report it comes from
• The deck ends with a conversation starter
• Briefings are built from current sports news and refreshed three times a day

FOUR KINDS OF CONVERSATION
• Cocktail Party: broad stories that are easy to bring up anywhere
• Sports Talk for Moms: warm prompts for talking with a sports-obsessed kid
• Office Watercooler: safe, current topics for coworkers
• Date Night: one good story and a follow-up question

WHAT IT IS NOT
• No live scores, betting picks or fantasy advice
• It covers the stories people mention, not every game or event
• Briefings are in English

PRIVACY
• No account, no ads and no cross-app tracking
• Your last briefing is saved on your iPhone, so it opens without a connection

FREE AND PRO
The Cocktail Party briefing is free every day, with one refresh a day, and does not expire. Pro is an optional upgrade, as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. It adds:
• All four conversation types
• Refresh any time, with new briefings up to three times a day

Subscriptions include a free trial for eligible new subscribers, and the app shows its length before you start. Prices appear in the app before you buy and vary by region. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions.
