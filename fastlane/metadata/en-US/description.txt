Gist makes sports easy to follow in five minutes a day.

Open one short briefing and get exactly what you need:

- What happened, in plain English.
- Why people care about it.
- One useful thing to say out loud.
- The backstory if someone asks a follow-up.
- A real source link for every story.
- One question that keeps the conversation going.

Gist is built for busy people who want the story without scores tables, betting noise, fantasy advice, or an endless news feed.

Choose the room you are walking into. The free Cocktail Party briefing covers the essential stories. Gist Pro adds separate briefings for work, family, and date night, refreshed morning, midday, and evening.

FREE AND PRO
