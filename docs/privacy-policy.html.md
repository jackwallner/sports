## Gist Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Gist provides sports conversation briefings without accounts, social features, ads, or behavioral tracking.

## Information used

The app does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, health data, personal prompts, or search history. It fetches cached briefings and story artwork from Supabase. When a story has no stored artwork, the app requests a generated illustration from the Pollinations image service (image.pollinations.ai); that request carries only a description of the scene, never anything about you. Network requests may include an IP address, request time, user agent, and response status for security and reliability.

## AI and sources

Briefings are generated server-side from curated public sports sources and cached before the app fetches them. The app does not send personal prompts to Gemini. Supabase and Google Gemini may retain server requests under their own policies.

## Purchases

Gist Pro purchases are processed by Apple on iPhone or Google Play on Android, and verified by RevenueCat. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user ID, transaction and entitlement information, and limited technical context, plus in-app upgrade statistics: how many times and when the upgrade screen was shown, how many app opens came before it, and which screen led to a purchase. It does not receive your personal sports preferences. See [RevenueCat's privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).

## Sharing, retention, and deletion

We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising. We retain service logs and purchase records only as needed for security, operations, support, and transaction processing. Contact us for a privacy request. Apple, Google, Supabase, Pollinations, and RevenueCat apply their own retention policies.

## Children and changes

Gist is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We may update this policy when the app or its services change.
