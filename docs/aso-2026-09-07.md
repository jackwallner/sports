# September 7, 2026 App Store positioning update

Target: Gist 1.1.3 (55), all 50 App Store Connect text locales, release automatically after approval.

## Decision

Gist becomes **Sports Cheat Sheet: Daily News**, subtitle **Be Ready for the Conversation**. Target people who want enough sports context to join a conversation, not a comprehensive sports-news reader. Explain stories, backstory, why they matter and what to say. Disclose English briefings and the free daily room versus Pro rooms.

English was decided first. Luna 5.6 max localizes the full proposition for the remaining stores. Existing binary labels and purchase product names are unchanged; this is a metadata-only refresh. Earlier incremental drafts are superseded.

## Evidence

ASC browser totals, June 9-September 6: 21 first-time downloads and 9,479 impressions. These are separate measures, not Apple's conversion-rate denominator.

July 13-26 had 0 downloads / 628 impressions; July 28-August 10 had 6 / 1,474. Low counts and other changes prevent causal attribution. Astro sports briefing #10 and sports news #33 suggest a clearer briefing proposition rather than another major brand pivot.

The signed-in Apple Ads account has no campaign for this app. All 11 account campaigns were paused or on hold in the inspected view. No paid campaign or spend was started. Detailed ASC API reports are incomplete or privacy-suppressed and were not treated as zero-download evidence. Earlier om97/lm97 analyses were hypotheses, checked against current source data.

## Verification

Existing processed TestFlight build 55 is attached to the new review draft. The app test suite passed on a leased headless simulator; no production RevenueCat key was configured. No app source changes were needed for this positioning update. Strict upload validates exact character limits, all 50 locale sets, privacy and EULA links, then compares every uploaded field against ASC. Review notes describe current free, paid and hardware flows. Current subscription prices and eligible seven-day trials were verified; no pricing changes were made.

Approved screenshots are preserved. Text localization does not change the app's interface language or imply localized editorial content.
